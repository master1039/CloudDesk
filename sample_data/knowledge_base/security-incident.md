# Suspected compromise

Treat successful sign-ins from unexpected locations as a security incident. Preserve audit evidence, recommend session revocation and credential rotation, and route the case to the security team.

## Restrictions

Do not declare the account safe and do not expose internal risk signals to an unverified requester.
