# MFA recovery

Use the approved identity-verification flow and check for an existing recovery method. Do not ask the customer to send backup codes, passwords, or identity documents in a ticket.

## Reset

An authorized administrator may initiate an MFA reset after verification. Revoke old sessions and enroll a new factor.
