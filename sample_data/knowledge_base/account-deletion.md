# Deletion requests

Verify the requester's authority, identify retention or legal-hold obligations, and record the exact requested scope. Deletion is irreversible and cannot be performed from an ordinary support reply.

## Escalation

Route privacy or GDPR erasure requests to the privacy team for approval and tracked execution.
