# Quota checks

Compare used bytes with the subscription limit. Deleted files in trash and retained versions can continue to consume storage until their retention period ends.

## Resolution

Empty trash, remove unnecessary versions, or ask an administrator to upgrade the plan. Do not delete customer files on their behalf.
